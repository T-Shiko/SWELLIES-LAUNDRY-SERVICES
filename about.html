Brief History

Swellies Laundry Services was started as a small local business in Swellendam to provide affordable and convenient laundry services to the community. The business was established after identifying a need for reliable washing, drying, ironing, and folding services for people who may not have enough time to do their laundry themselves.

The business initially started by serving a small number of local customers and has gradually aimed to expand its services to families, students, working individuals, and small businesses. As the business grows, Swellies Laundry Services is now introducing a website to improve its online presence, make its services easier to find, and allow customers to access information and contact the business more conveniently.

Mission Statement

Swellies Laundry Services’ mission is to provide affordable, reliable, and convenient laundry services to people in the local community while delivering excellent customer service and taking care of customers’ clothing.

Vision Statement

Swellies Laundry Services’ vision is to become the most trusted and convenient laundry service in Swellendam and beyond, while growing the business and making laundry easier for individuals, families, students, and small businesses.
