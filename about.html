Who We Are

Swellies Laundry Services is a local laundry business concept based in Swellendam, Western Cape. Our aim is to make laundry easier for individuals, families and busy professionals by offering convenient and affordable laundry solutions.

Our Story

Swellies Laundry Services was developed from the idea of helping people who struggle to find enough time to wash, dry and care for their clothing.

We aim to provide a convenient solution for the Swellendam community through services such as washing, drying, ironing, folding, dry cleaning, and collection and delivery.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide affordable, reliable and convenient laundry services while delivering excellent customer care.

Our Vision

Our vision is to become the most trusted laundry service in Swellendam and beyond, making laundry easier for everyone.
